STYLE (every image): the look of a classic late-1990s Korean 2D online fantasy RPG, remastered in high resolution
- A cosy, nostalgic, gentle and cute online fantasy world full of friendly adventurers: 2D character sprites walking over hand-painted 3D fields, seen from about 45 degrees above.
- Everything looks like classic hand-pixelled 2D game sprites redrawn cleanly at high resolution (smooth, not blocky): small, precise shapes; soft 3- or 4-tone shading per material with a light dithered texture; thin outlines in a darker shade of each colour (dark brown on skin and leather, dark navy on blue cloth), never thick black lines.
- NOT a modern mobile-game look: no glossy plastic rendering, no airbrushed gradients, no thick outlines, no 3D render, no flat vector style.
- Colours: a muted, slightly desaturated medieval-fantasy palette (dusty navy, slate blue, khaki, leather brown, ivory, moss green, burgundy, old brass), soft warm daylight from the upper left.
- Every design is ORIGINAL. Do not copy any existing game's characters, monsters, items, logos or interface, and do not use any existing game's name.
- Icons look like the small inventory and skill icons of that era, remastered: one bold, simple object or symbol with an instantly readable silhouette, 3-tone shading, thin dark outlines, muted colours with one bright accent.

SHEET RULES (every sheet)
- Square 1:1 image: a grid of 4 columns x 4 rows of equal square cells = 16 icons, numbered left to right, top to bottom (1-4 the top row, 13-16 the bottom row).
- Background: a perfectly flat, solid colour everywhere (given for each sheet); never use that colour, or anything close to it, inside the icons.
- Each icon is ONE object or symbol alone, centred in its cell and filling about 70% of it, with NO tile, panel, frame, badge, background colour or glow behind it, and plenty of plain background between icons; nothing touching the cell edges or the image edge.
- No text, numbers or letters anywhere.
- Within a sheet, give every icon a clearly different shape and colour so no two look alike.
