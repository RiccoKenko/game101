# Audio credits

Every sound in `public/audio/` was made from the sources below. Sound effects were trimmed, layered,
filtered and normalized; music tracks were cut into seamless loops and loudness-normalized.

All sources are CC0 (public domain) except **Waltz of the Ghosts**, which is CC-BY 4.0 and requires the
attribution shown below (it is also shown in the game's README).
