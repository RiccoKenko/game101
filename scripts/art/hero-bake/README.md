# Hero sprite bake

Bakes Little Archer (and Little Mage) from a rigged 2D cut-out puppet in the old Three.js project (branch
`sky-pirates-td`) into an 8-column grid of 256x256 frames plus a JSON of the animations:

- `archer` (default): the redesigned "Feather Cap" archer, a new puppet on the old hero's skeleton, baked to
  `art/baked/hero/hero.png` and `hero.json`.
- `mage`: Little Mage, the second hero, on the same skeleton and the same animation table, baked to
  `art/baked/mage/hero.png` and `hero.json` (the game sheet is `public/art/hero/mage.webp` + `mage.json`).
- `hero`: the old anime hero, as first baked (now kept in `art/baked/hero_anime/`).

None of these files are part of this game's build. Their folders mirror the `sky-pirates-td` checkout: copy them
over it and they add or replace these files there.

- `dev/bake.html` + `src/dev/bake.ts`: a page that renders only the puppet on a transparent WebGL canvas
  (orthographic camera with its origin at the feet, facing right, fixed 1/240 s substeps, no random blinks) and
  hands back raw premultiplied pixels and bone transforms.
- `scripts/bake-hero.mjs`: starts Vite and drives the page in headless Chromium. It renders at 4x and averages
  down in premultiplied space, so edges have no dark or white fringe. Then it checks the frames and writes the
  sheet. The checks: nothing touches a frame edge, loops close, and run, runaim and runrelease share their body
  bones exactly.
- `src/puppet/clips/archer.ts` (+ `index.ts`): the archer's clips. These are the hero clips retuned for chibi
  proportions, plus `aim`, `loose`, `runAim` and `runRelease`. `src/puppet/clips/mage.ts`: the mage's clips
  (`ready`, `run`, `runCharge`, `runCast`, `cast`, `hit`, `ko`, `cheer`).
- `scripts/art/slice.ts`, `slicer.ts`, `sheets.json`: the slicer, with two new options (`keep` polygons and
  `clearColour` blobs) and the `archer` entry: part boxes, cuts, blush and pink-spill removal on the eyes, the
  cape split into a collar and a body, and a bare near forearm lifted from the rig pose (sheet `extra`). For the
  mage's green-screen sheets, a third option, `despill` (un-mix the #00FF00 left on part edges, `despillKey`), and
  the `mage` entry.
- `art/rigs/archer.json`: the archer rig, generated by `tools/fit/gen_archer.py` from part fits against the rig
  pose (`tools/fit/fits.json`, made with `fitarch.py`) and measured joints. `art/rigs/mage.json` likewise from
  `tools/fit/gen_mage.py` and `fits_mage.json` (`fitarch.py` fits the character named by `CHAR`, default archer;
  `pegcut.py` printed the mage's peg-cut polygons).
- `scripts/shot-rig.mjs`: screenshots `dev/rig.html`, e.g. the rest pose over the rig pose
  (`"c=archer&overlay=0.45"`).

## Re-run (archer)

```sh
git -C /home/user/game101 fetch origin sky-pirates-td
git -C /home/user/game101 worktree add --detach /tmp/sp origin/sky-pirates-td   # any path
cd /tmp/sp && npm ci                      # Playwright's Chromium is preinstalled at /opt/pw-browsers/chromium
cp -r /home/user/game101/scripts/art/hero-bake/{dev,src,scripts,art} /tmp/sp/
mkdir -p art/incoming/archer
for s in master rigpose head_faces parts_body hands_props; do
  cp /home/user/game101/art/incoming/mr/hero_$s.png art/incoming/archer/archer_$s.png
done
cp art/incoming/archer/archer_rigpose.png art/incoming/archer/archer_extra.png   # source of the near forearm
npm run art -- archer                     # slice into art/sliced/archer/ (look at the *.check.png)
npm run art:publish -- archer             # public/sprites/archer/ with art/rigs/archer.json
node scripts/shot-rig.mjs /tmp/overlay.png "c=archer&overlay=0.45&height=1300&cell=850" 900 1300
node scripts/bake-hero.mjs /tmp/archer-out --char archer --contact /tmp/archer-check
cp /tmp/archer-out/hero.png /tmp/archer-out/hero.json /home/user/game101/art/baked/hero/
```

Look at `/tmp/archer-check/contact.png` before copying. It shows every frame on mid grey, with the frame border,
the baseline and the feet point. Also look at `run_strip.png`: run, runaim and runrelease, frame i under frame i.
The log lists:

- each animation's extents and the lowest row in each frame;
- any frame touching the edge (`PROBLEM ...`);
- for loops, the difference between "one more step" and the first frame (0.000 = seamless);
- the largest difference in body-bone transforms between run, runaim and runrelease (must be 0).

`PROBE=1` renders on 512 px frames and prints every frame's box relative to the feet. Use it to check whether
a change would fit. `ONLY=ko,cheer` limits a run to some animations.

To change the rig, edit `tools/fit/gen_archer.py` (joints, part mappings, z order) and run it from the checkout
root with `python3 <path>/gen_archer.py`. It needs numpy and Pillow, and `art/sliced/archer/` from the slicer.
Then publish again.

## What is baked (archer)

Frames are 256x256 with feet at [128, 236] and the hero facing right. He stands ~201 px tall from cap top to
soles (`K` = 0.158 frame px per rig px).

| animation | frames | fps | loop | from (`src/puppet/clips/archer.ts`) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| idle | 0-15 | 12 | yes | `aim`: full draw, arrow nocked, breathing (one breath per 1.33 s) |
| run | 16-23 | 16 | yes | `run`: one 0.5 s stride, bow held low |
| runaim | 24-31 | 16 | yes | `runAim`: run's body frame for frame, arms at full draw |
| runrelease | 32-39 | 16 | yes | `runRelease`: run's body frame for frame, string hand flung back, no arrow |
| shoot | 40-47 | 16 | no | `loose`: release, then re-draw; ends on idle frame 0 |
| hurt | 48-53 | 12 | no | `hit` |
| ko | 54-63 | 12 | no | `ko`, last frame lying on his back |
| cheer | 64-75 | 12 | no | `cheer` up to its arms-up hold |

For the old hero, run `node scripts/bake-hero.mjs <out> --char hero`. It reproduces `art/baked/hero_anime/`
`hero.png` and `hero_menu_aim.png` (written as `hero_menu.png`) byte for byte. With `MENU_CLIP=idle` the menu still
is the calm `hero_menu.png`.

## Re-run (mage)

In the same checkout, with this folder copied over it as for the archer:

```sh
mkdir -p art/incoming/mage
for s in master rigpose head_faces parts_body hands_props; do
  cp /home/user/game101/art/incoming/mr/mage_$s.png art/incoming/mage/
done
npm run art -- mage                       # slice into art/sliced/mage/ (look at the *.check.png)
python3 /home/user/game101/scripts/art/hero-bake/tools/fit/gen_mage.py   # only after changing the rig
npm run art:publish -- mage               # public/sprites/mage/ with art/rigs/mage.json
node scripts/shot-rig.mjs /tmp/mage-overlay.png "c=mage&overlay=0.45&height=1450&cell=900" 900 1400
node scripts/bake-hero.mjs /tmp/mage-out --char mage --contact /tmp/mage-check
mkdir -p /home/user/game101/art/baked/mage
cp /tmp/mage-out/hero.png /tmp/mage-out/hero.json /home/user/game101/art/baked/mage/
# The game sheet: 192 px frames (feet [96, 177]), same animation table.
node -e "
const sharp = require('sharp'), fs = require('fs'), G = '/home/user/game101';
const meta = JSON.parse(fs.readFileSync(G + '/art/baked/mage/hero.json', 'utf8'));
sharp(G + '/art/baked/mage/hero.png').resize(1536, 1920, { kernel: 'lanczos3' }).webp({ quality: 92 })
  .toFile(G + '/public/art/hero/mage.webp').then(() => fs.writeFileSync(G + '/public/art/hero/mage.json',
  JSON.stringify({ frameWidth: 192, frameHeight: 192, feet: [96, 177], animations: meta.animations }, null, 2) + '\\n'));
"
```

The sheets have a flat green background: `removeBackground` keys it like the magenta ones (enclosed pockets go
too), and `despill: 3` turns the green left on edge pixels (and strongly green specks inside the art) back into
soft edges of the art's own colour. Pegs (the skin-coloured stubs at joint ends) are cut away with polygons.
Look at `contact.png`, `run_strip.png` and the overlay as for the archer. The log also prints, for every frame,
where the near hand's palm, the pointing fingertip and the staff's crystal are, from the feet with y up, in 256 px
frames and in the game's 192 px frames.

To refit a part, e.g. the head (sheet px -> rig-pose px; the jobs used for `fits_mage.json`):

```sh
CHAR=mage python3 <hero-bake>/tools/fit/fitarch.py <hero-bake>/tools/fit/fits_mage.json '{
  "head": {"name": "head", "g": [360, 270, 1.15, 0]},
  "eyes-open": {"name": "eyes-open", "g": [420, 370, 1.1, 0]},
  "shin#far": {"name": "shin", "g": [505, 1235, 1.0, 0], "kw": {"region": [800, 705, 990, 965]}}}'
```

`gen_mage.py` prints the staff's and the near hand's rest angles; `mage.ts` keeps them as `STAFF_REST` and
`NEAR_HAND_REST` (update them if the hand or staff mapping changes).

## What is baked (mage)

Frames are 256x256 with feet at [128, 236], facing right. She stands ~201 px from hat top to soles (`K` = 0.144
frame px per rig px; the trimmed rig pose is 1366 rig px tall). Same table as the archer, so the game swaps the
sheets with no code change:

| animation | frames | fps | loop | from (`src/puppet/clips/mage.ts`) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| idle | 0-15 | 12 | yes | `ready`: staff upright in the far hand (foot on the ground), near hand palm-up at the chest, breathing (one breath per 1.33 s), braid, hat tip and capelet swaying |
| run | 16-23 | 16 | yes | `run`: one 0.5 s stride, staff carried slanted (crystal forward), near arm pumping |
| runaim | 24-31 | 16 | yes | `runCharge`: run's body frame for frame, near hand out at chest height palm-out, staff tipped forward |
| runrelease | 32-39 | 16 | yes | `runCast`: run's body frame for frame, near arm thrust all the way out, staff pushed forward, shouting |
| shoot | 40-47 | 16 | no | `cast`: thrust on frame 0 (the bolt flies), overshoot, settle; ends on idle frame 0 |
| hurt | 48-53 | 12 | no | `hit`: flinch, hurt eyes, the staff wobbles back |
| ko | 54-63 | 12 | no | `ko`: totters and falls on her back, KO eyes; the staff tips forward and ends lying across her |
| cheer | 64-75 | 12 | no | `cheer` up to its hold: hop, staff thrust up high, other hand flung out, happy eyes and open mouth |

How the rig is put together (`gen_mage.py`):

- The sleeve is two pieces: the sheet's bell sleeves (`sleeve-a`/`-b`, the parts sheet's "upper arms") are the
  forearms, cuffs at the wrist, drawn over the plain sleeve tubes (`tube-a`/`-b`, cut of their pegs and cuffs) as
  upper arms, so a hanging arm reads as the rig pose's single bell sleeve. Hands are drawn over the cuffs; the
  open casting hand is mirrored (palm up, fingers forward, thumb up). The bracelet comes with the near hands.
- The staff is fixed in the far fist, running through the hole the sheet drew, so the clips aim it by turning the
  far hand (`staffAt`). The far arm, hand and staff sit behind the body; the casting forearm and hand go in front
  of the capelet when raised (`z` overrides).
- The rig pose is three-quarter, so the near shoulder sits far behind the chest: the clips slide it forward under
  the capelet when ready (45 rig px) and casting (150), and stretch the arm a little on the thrust.
- The braid (`braid` bone, where the archer's feather was) and the hat tip (`hatTip`, split off the head piece with
  an overlap) swing on springs, as does the cape body (`capelet`); the collar stays on the torso. Both boots point
  forward with their soles on the ground (the rig pose turns the near one out and lifts the far one).
